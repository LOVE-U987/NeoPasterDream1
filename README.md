## 🎬  This is a dream.

</div>

> You are a ​**Dreamseeker**​.
> 
> One day, you discover a **natural wonder** jutting out from reality in the Overworld. Curiosity draws you closer, and in the sleep that follows, you forge a connection with "that place."
> 
> The journey through the dream begins the moment you ​**close your eyes**​.
> 
> You will traverse notes and diaries left by Dreamseekers before you, learning **knowledge** and gaining the power to ​**control the dream**​. You will find treasures in ancient ruins, forge weapons with special functions, and explore the secrets at the very source of this dream—
> 
> **Even though those who came before are long gone.**
> 
> Because by now, ​**there is no turning back**​.

## ⚔️ Combat is Not a Reskin · It's Another Set of Rules

</div>

### 🌀 Blink Technique

Built-in **dodge** skill. The core of Souls-like displacement.

> It's not a cheat that teleports you ten blocks away with one press. It's a ​**dodge**​.

---

### 🔮 Spell System · Independent Operation — Split into an addon mod

Five-element spell projectiles + staff projectile families + healing/rage fields.

> Fireballs, Ice Spikes, Shadow Arrows—build your build. Staffs are weapons, not decorations.

---

### 🧠 Sanity System · Independent Operation — Split into an addon mod

Witnessing the unspeakable, sanity slips away.

> The screen will ​**shake**​. Vision will ​**darken**​. You will hear ​**things you shouldn't**​. Too low, and nightmares will find you.

---

### ⚡ Dream Fusion Energy System · Independent Operation — Split into an addon mod

Independent energy gauge. Staffs consume it, rings recharge it.

> Not experience. Not hunger. It is your ​**connection strength to the dream**​. Energy depleted, spells fall silent.

---

### 🛠️ Weapon Workshop

Anvil, Forge, Cooling Basin, Grindstone ​**four-piece set + three GUI sets**​.

> **Forge** your exclusive weapons

---

### 💍 Accessory System

Deep Curios integration. Rings, bracelets, amulets, paper airplanes, and ​**50+ accessories**​.

> Stats aligned with vanilla values
